Welcome, Operator

A restricted access gateway is guarded by a client-side session check.

Search the server for partner authentication material and manipulate your browser cookies to bypass the gateway perimeter.

Challenge Objectives

  • Inspect robots.txt to uncover internal application endpoints.
  • Visit the partner portal and extract the encoded session token.
  • Decode the Base64 token to reveal the required cookie key-value pair.
  • Navigate to the restricted /secure/ gateway and inject the cookie via Browser DevTools.
  • Bypass the 403 access barrier to unlock the confidential flag.
